Frequently Asked Questions

A few things people usually ask about this page.

What is OPM?

OPM, or Original Pilipino Music, covers songs written and performed by Filipino artists, from the Manila Sound of the seventies to today's bedroom pop and rap.

How were the genres and songs chosen?

Each genre on this site was picked and curated by a different group member, based on their own personal recommendations rather than official charts or rankings.

Is this an official or commercial project?

No, it was created as a lab activity under our professor, Dr. Ramiro Z. Dela Cruz.
