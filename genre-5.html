Genre: Pinoy Hip-Hop

Pinoy Hip-Hop Recommendation

Pinoy hip-hop took the genre's global template and localized it, mixing Tagalog and regional slang over local beats: a scene that grew from underground battles into a mainstream streaming force. This list gathers the tracks that best show off that homegrown wordplay and swagger.

Popular

    A track from a rapper who built his name in Fliptop's underground battle rap scene.
