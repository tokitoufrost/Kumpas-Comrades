Genre: Indie

Filipino Indie Recommendation

Filipino indie grew out of bedrooms, dorm rooms, and small local scenes rather than major labels: lo-fi production, diary-like lyrics, and a DIY spirit that streaming made possible nationwide. This list gathers tracks that capture that intimate, homegrown sound.

Popular

  1. 1
    Bawat Piyesa Munimuni

    A reflective folk-rock track from a band known for poetic, literature-leaning lyricism.

  2. 2
    Hiwaga Tatin DC

    A moody, mystery-tinged track that captures the intimate, homegrown side of Filipino indie.

  3. 3
    Paraluman Adie

    One of the breakout indie hits of the streaming era, turning Adie into a household name.

  4. 4
    Nakauwi Na Ang Bandang Shirley

    A wistful "coming home" song that's become a favorite among younger indie listeners.

  5. 5
    Mahika Adie, Janine Berdin

    A duet follow-up that rode the wave of "Paraluman," pairing two of indie OPM's biggest voices.
