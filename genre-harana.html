Genre: Harana

Branco's Recommendation

Harana is the traditional Filipino courtship serenade, sung by a suitor beneath a woman's window, usually with nothing but an acoustic guitar. It grew out of the Spanish colonial era and stayed alive through the mid-20th century, prizing sincerity and quiet longing over spectacle. This list gathers the songs that carry that spirit best: soft, guitar-led, and unmistakably OPM.

Popular

  1. 1
    Ipagpatawad Mo VST & Company

    A heartfelt plea for forgiveness that became one of OPM's most-covered apology ballads.

  2. 2
    Harana Parokya ni Edgar

    A cheeky tribute to the serenade tradition from a band better known for comedic rock.

  3. 3
    Ligaya Eraserheads

    A dreamy love song from 1997's "Cutterpillow," one of the most acclaimed OPM albums ever released.

  4. 4
    Panalangin APO Hiking Society

    A gentle, prayer-like ballad from a trio considered one of OPM's founding acts.

  5. 5
    Miss Universe ng Buhay Ko Hotdog

    A playful love song from Hotdog, the band widely credited with pioneering the 1970s "Manila Sound."
